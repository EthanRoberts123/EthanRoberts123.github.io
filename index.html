About Me

Turning Spatial Data Into Insights

I am currently completing my BSc (Hons) in Geoinformatics at Stellenbosch University, my academic focus is split between advanced remote sensing (including SAR and hyperspectral analysis), geocomputation, and spatial modeling. My current research combines the use of spatial data science and agriculture, leveraging high-resolution multispectral imagery and machine learning workflows to solve complex challenges within viticulture.
